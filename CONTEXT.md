# Portfolio

Marcus's personal portfolio site, presenting him to recruiters as a software engineer focused on backend and data engineering.

## Language

**Visitor**:
A recruiter or hiring manager reading the site; the audience every page is written for.
_Avoid_: user, customer

**Featured project**:
A piece of work presented on the site to show what Marcus can do.
_Avoid_: portfolio item, showcase

**Headline**:
The job title and pitch the site leads with; the first thing a visitor reads. Its title and keywords match Marcus's LinkedIn and CV, while the pitch may be more personal than those.

**Title**:
The job title in the headline, naming the role Marcus wants recruiters to call him about.
_Avoid_: tagline, role

**Pitch**:
The short text under the title, a sentence or two, that says what makes Marcus worth calling.
_Avoid_: tagline, slogan, bio

**Case study**:
A written account of a featured project that cannot be shown or run as-is (for example, internal work automations), describing the problem, approach and outcome.
_Avoid_: write-up, blog post

**Demo**:
A featured project the visitor can open and use directly.

**Other work**:
A project linked in a single line below the featured projects, with no case study of its own.
_Avoid_: side projects, misc

**Home server**:
Marcus's own Ubuntu machine that hosts the site behind a Cloudflare Tunnel.
_Avoid_: VPS, cloud host
